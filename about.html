OUR MISSION

To equip and build up people to be demonstrators of the knowledge of Christ and His Church-life to the world.

Metamorphoo is God's wisdom to reach unbelievers and unstable new creations to grow in the all-inclusiveness of God.

It's the practical grounds of demonstrating the Church-life to the CHURCH.

At Metamorphoo, everyone is seen as a minister (steward or custodian) of the revelation of Jesus and we complement each other to fulfill God's plans and purposes for our lives.

OUR VISION

That all will believe in the power of the gospel and it's transformative ability in the lives of the saints.

Every heart will be filled and saturated with the intrinsic knowledge of the fullness of the Reigning life in Christ and to establish the global Church in her essence.

As the global church rises in her essence, we anticipate:

A tidal wave of revival and awakening.

A harvest of souls and transformed lives.

A radiant display of God's glory and presence.

OUR VALUES

COMPASSION AND BROTHERLY LOVE: We believe in, and demonstrate empathy, brotherly love and kindness,towards one another.

INCLUSIVITY AND ONENESS: We believe that everyone has what it takes to bring transformation in the lives of people and the world in their own way. Hence, we give opportunity to everyone to function and serve.

INTEGRITY AND GENUINENESS: We serve the Lord's agenda with genuineness and intergrity of heart, transparency, honesty and accountability.

DARINGNESS & COLLABORATION: We're daring to take big steps for the Lord. We partner with each other to impact the world.
